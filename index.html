About this model

Curry lines are not scientifically established. There is no scientifically verified evidence that they represent real energy lines on Earth.

Currykartan visualizes a theoretical, esoteric model. Mathematics, coordinates and map projection are used to draw the model, not to prove it.

The map geometry itself is computed in SWEREF 99 TM (EPSG:3006) and is technically accurate; the existence of the lines is not claimed.
